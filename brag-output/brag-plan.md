# Brag Plan: Chef Jay's

## What is this app?
A storefront for a one-man drinks and catering business in Elizabeth, New Jersey: nine
piña coladas and a Ghanaian kitchen, ordered by cart or by WhatsApp, launching with a
grand opening offer that applies itself at checkout.

## The angle
This is not a joke product, so the video does not wink. It is a grand-opening film for a
neighbourhood drinks brand: real bottles, real labels, real prices, and the offer landing
in a real cart. The brag is that a one-man operation has a storefront that behaves like a
proper one — the discount is computed, not advertised.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The hero photograph and the line the site actually opens with: **"Piña colada, by Chef
Jay."** Held, not rushed. The drink earns the next twenty seconds before a single claim
is made.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The lineup arriving one bottle at a time — Classic $4.99, Mango $5.99, Tigernut $6.99,
  Gold Coast Corn Shake $6.99 — each a real product photograph with its real price.
- Four bottles in the cart, and the discount line writing itself in:
  `Grand opening — 4th bottle free  −$4.99`. Subtotal strikes, total lands at $23.97.
- The kitchen: Kpataashie, with one dish name under it.

## Outro / punchline
No punchline — a sign-off. The wordmark, the dates, the address. The restraint is the
point: the site is open, that is the whole announcement.

## User flow worth showing
Entry → key action → result:
1. **Entry:** the shop, "The fridge", bottles on a shelf.
2. **Key action:** a fourth bottle is added to the cart.
3. **Result:** the grand opening line appears in the totals and takes $4.99 off.

Scene 3 is the centrepiece. It is the product doing its thing rather than describing it.

## Tone
- Preset: `polished`
- Creative direction: a quiet grand-opening film for a neighbourhood drinks brand
- Interpretation: four scenes, long holds, soft crossfades, no caps, no exclamation. The
  photography carries the energy; the typography stays still and lets it.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 21.8 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#fdf8ef` (cream), with `#ecdcc4` (sand) for the offer scene
- Accent: `#b4472c` (clay) — the one accent the site allows itself
- Text: `#241a12` (bark), secondary `#574636` (bark-soft), faint `#8a7660`
- Display font: Fraunces (variable, SOFT 20 / WONK 1, -0.02em tracking)
- Body font: Plus Jakarta Sans
- Strongest visual element: the product photographs — bottle and hurricane glass on a
  travertine ledge against sunlit limewash, one fruit per flavour

## Share copy (draft)
Chef Jay's is open: nine piña coladas, a Ghanaian kitchen, and a grand opening offer that
takes itself off at checkout. chefjayskp.com

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed, low and continuous, never the subject
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (110 BPM, warmest and
  slowest of the bundled set)
- Music treatment: start at 0:00, sit low under the whole piece, gentle fade-in over the
  first beat and a fade-out across the final second. No ducking, no build.
- Music cue guidance: preset read from `assets/music/cues/`. Strong cues at **8.74s**,
  **17.47s**, **18.56s**. Beat grid every ~0.55s. Target 8.74s for the fourth bottle
  landing in the cart and 17.47s for the kitchen cut. Sequential reveals snap to *every
  other* beat (~1.1s apart) so each line is readable.
- Audio-reactive treatment: none. A still, confident piece; reactive motion would cheapen it.
- SFX posture: sparse. Three at most, all motion-matched: a soft interface tick per bottle
  arriving, one quiet confirmation when the discount line lands, nothing else.
- Audio-coupled moments: the four bottles arriving one by one; the discount line appearing
  in the totals.
- Restraint rule: no whooshes, no risers, no impacts. If a sound is noticed as a sound, it
  is wrong.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook: the hero — 4.4s
Full-bleed `public/images/hero.jpg` (tall glass, pineapple wedge, palm shadow on limewash).
A cream scrim from the left, as on the real site. Display type sets in over it:
**"Piña colada,"** then **"by Chef Jay"** in clay on the second line. Both hold still.
Sequential/interaction: two-line headline, second line 0.5s after the first, then a full
2.5s hold on the complete line.
Audio intent: the bed arrives at a low level; nothing else.
Audio-coupled idea: none — the hook is silent apart from the music.
Music: warm, low, already running.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The lineup — 5.4s
Cream ground. Small eyebrow "Eight coladas, one corn shake", then four real product cards
arrive one at a time: Classic Piña Colada $4.99, Mango $5.99, Tigernut $6.99, Gold Coast
Corn Shake $6.99. Each card is the actual photograph from `public/images/` with the name
and price beneath in the site's own type.
Sequential/interaction: yes — four cards, one per two beats (~1.1s apart), each settling
before the next. All four hold together for the last ~1s.
Audio intent: light punctuation, so each arrival feels placed rather than animated.
Audio-coupled idea: a soft interface tick on each card.
Music: steady, unchanged.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The offer lands — 6.6s
Sand ground. A cart panel recreated from the real one: four line items (2 × Classic,
Mango, Tigernut), then the totals block. Subtotal $22.96 sits for a beat. On the strong
cue, the clay line **"Grand opening — 4th bottle free   −$4.99"** writes in, and the total
resolves to **$23.97**. A single line beneath: "No code. It comes off by itself."
Sequential/interaction: yes — line items are already present; the discount row appears on
the 8.74s strong cue, then the total number counts from $28.96 to $23.97 over 0.4s.
Audio intent: one quiet confirmation at the moment the discount appears. Nothing triumphant.
Audio-coupled idea: the discount row landing; the total ticking down.
Music: steady.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — The kitchen, then the sign-off — 5.4s
`public/images/kpataashie.jpg` full-bleed with a bark scrim, "Kpataashie" and beneath it
in italics "Ga for kitchen · jollof, fried rice, goat soups". Holds 2.6s, then dissolves to
cream for the sign-off: **Chef Jay's**, then in small tracked caps
"GRAND OPENING · 3 – 18 OCTOBER" and "chefjayskp.com".
Sequential/interaction: wordmark first, then the two small lines together 0.6s later.
Audio intent: the bed thins and fades out across the last second.
Audio-coupled idea: none.
Music: fade to silence by the final frame.
Transition mood: final — fade to cream

**Music mood for this video:** warm, unhurried, confident — business-open music, not launch-hype music.
**Audio summary:** One low warm bed from first frame to last, two soft interface ticks under the lineup, one quiet confirmation when the discount lands, then a clean fade — the sound of a shop opening its door rather than an advert shouting.
